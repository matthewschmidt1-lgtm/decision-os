# Module 3 proposal v2: review

Reviewed: `docs/module3-proposal-v2.md` against `scratchpad/books.md` (the author's own takeaways), `js/scenarios2.js`, `CLAUDE.md`, `js/lessons/index.js` and `js/lessons/pictures.js`. No code or proposal text was changed. Everything marked "verified" was checked by script (section 5) or by direct comparison of quoted text; everything else is marked as judgment.

## 1. Verdict

1. **Not ready to port yet.** The data is structurally clean (every mechanical check in 1b passes), but three design decisions have to be made first, and about a dozen cases need copy fixes.
2. **Problem 1: the strongest answer is the longest in 27 of 30 cases** and is usually three or four moves stapled together ("Say what it's for, cut to three fields, promise Monday replies, hold Friday."). The 90-character cap did not fix what CLAUDE.md's rule is for.
3. **Problem 2: most pictures decorate the feedback instead of drawing the algorithm.** Sixteen of thirty place the four options on an invented ordinal line ("position only"); the four belief bars, which the layout shows before the pick, end on the answer in three cases, which breaks the site's "never reveal the preferred option before the choice" constraint.
4. **Problem 3: the "values you lean on" read-out cannot be computed as specified.** Nine sound options lean on a value outside their case's `trade` pair, and only four pairs repeat across the module, so "candor over kindness in 6 of 8" can never be produced.
5. **Highest-value single fix:** rewrite every best label as one move under about 60 characters, moving the second and third clauses into `feedback`. It fixes the length tell, the double-barrelled labels and most of the Focus findings in one pass.
